Welcome to Varanasi

Varanasi is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world and is famous for its ghats and rich culture.

History

Varanasi has a long and fascinating history. The city has remained an important cultural and religious centre for centuries.

Famous Places

Some famous places include Dashashwamedh Ghat, Assi Ghat, Kashi Vishwanath Temple and Sarnath.

Culture

The city is famous for music, art, food, festivals and traditional Indian culture.
